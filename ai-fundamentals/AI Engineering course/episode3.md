# Episode-3 at AI Engineering 

## Topic Covered
### System Role & Temperature in Large Language Models (LLMs)

Today, I learned two fundamental concepts used while interacting with Large Language Models like GPT.

---

## System Role

The **System Role** defines the behavior and personality of the AI before the conversation begins.

It acts as an instruction that guides how the model should respond throughout the interaction.

### Examples
- You are a helpful AI tutor.
- You are a senior software engineer.
- You are an interviewer conducting technical interviews.
- You are a travel guide.

The system role helps maintain consistency in responses regardless of the user's prompts.

---

## Temperature

The **Temperature** parameter controls how random or creative the model's responses are.

- **Low Temperature (0.0 - 0.3)**
  - More deterministic
  - More accurate
  - Better for coding, math, debugging, and factual questions

- **Medium Temperature (0.5 - 0.7)**
  - Balanced between creativity and accuracy
  - Suitable for general conversations

- **High Temperature (0.8 - 1.5+)**
  - More creative
  - More diverse responses
  - Better for storytelling, brainstorming, and content generation
